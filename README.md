# Watch Fitness Tracker

A *fitness tracking application* developed during the *Spark the Summer Hackathon* in our Diploma 2nd year.  
The app was designed to promote healthy habits through activity tracking and personalized health insights.

## 📌 Features
- *Health Index Calculator* – Enter your body weight, height, and age to get a health status index (Good, Bad, Extreme Bad, etc.).
- *Sleep Reminder* – Sends notifications to remind users to maintain healthy sleep patterns.
- *Step Counter* – Tracks the number of steps taken during the day.
